Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about stream extraction, formats, and safety.

01. Is MediaSnap completely free to use?

Yes! MediaSnap is 100% free with unlimited conversions. There are no subscriptions, mandatory account sign-ups, or credit card requirements.

02. How does 320kbps MP3 extraction work without quality loss?

Our backend queries Google’s streaming servers directly for the highest source audio track (typically 160kbps Opus or 128kbps AAC) and transcodes it at 320kbps with full dynamic range preservation, ensuring that no frequency cutoff occurs.

03. Can I download YouTube Shorts on mobile devices?

Yes. Tap "Share" on any YouTube Short inside the YouTube app, copy the link, and paste it into MediaSnap on your mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox). It will immediately detect the vertical aspect ratio.

04. Where do my downloaded media files get saved?

Files are downloaded straight into your operating system's default Downloads directory. On iPhone / iPad, you can find them in the "Files" app under Downloads.

05. Do you store or track the links I download?

Never. We strictly follow a privacy-by-design policy. We don’t maintain database records of submitted links, user identifiers, or personal browsing telemetry.
